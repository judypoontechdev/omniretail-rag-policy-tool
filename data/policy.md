### 1.1 Statutory and Voluntarily Enhanced Return Rights

OmniRetail UK Ltd ("the Company") operates in strict accordance with the Consumer Rights Act 2015 and the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013. While the statutory "cooling-off" period for distance selling is 14 days, the Company provides an enhanced voluntary return window of 30 calendar days from the date of physical receipt of the goods. This policy applies to all standard retail transactions conducted via the OmniRetail UK e-commerce platform.

### 1.2 Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for a full refund under the 30-day "Change of Mind" policy, items must be returned in their "as-new" condition. This is defined as being in the original, undamaged retail packaging, with all factory seals intact, and all accessories, manuals, and bundled promotional items included. If an item is returned with broken seals or signs of handling beyond what is necessary to establish the nature, characteristics, and functioning of the goods, the Company reserves the right to deduct a diminished value fee, which may range from 20% to 50% of the original purchase price, pursuant to Regulation 34(9) of the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013.

### 1.3 Proof of Purchase Requirements

No return, exchange, or refund will be authorized without a valid Proof of Purchase. Acceptable forms include:

1. The original digital Order Confirmation email containing the 12-digit OMNI-UK transaction ID.
2. A physical packing slip included in the original shipment.
3. A valid VAT invoice issued by OmniRetail UK Ltd.
   Bank statements may be used to locate a transaction within our internal ERP system but do not constitute definitive proof of purchase for the purposes of initiating a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA). Customers must ensure that the serial number on the returned product matches the serial number recorded in the Company’s outbound logistics database at the time of dispatch.

### 2.1 Hygiene and Health Protection Exclusions

In accordance with Regulation 28(3)(a) of the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013, the right to cancel does not apply to the supply of sealed goods which are not suitable for return due to health protection or hygiene reasons and were unsealed after delivery. OmniRetail UK Ltd strictly enforces this exclusion to ensure the safety of our customers and staff.

### 2.2 Specific Product Categories

The following categories are strictly non-returnable once the primary hygiene seal or shrink-wrap has been tampered with or removed:

- **Cosmetics and Fragrances:** Any makeup, skincare, or perfume where the box seal or bottle pump has been activated.
- **Earrings and Body Jewellery:** Due to the risk of blood-borne pathogen transmission, these items are non-returnable once the tamper-evident bag is opened.
- **Underwear and Swimwear:** Items must be returned with the original hygiene strip intact. If the strip is removed or shows signs of re-application, the return will be rejected.
- **Personal Care Electronics:** Electric toothbrushes, shavers, and epilators cannot be returned once the box is opened.

### 2.3 Sealed Software and Media

Digital media, including video games, software licenses, and Blu-ray discs, are exempt from the 30-day return policy once the factory shrink-wrap is broken. This is to prevent the unauthorized copying of intellectual property. If the software is found to be defective, it will be replaced with an identical title but cannot be refunded for a change of mind.

### 2.4 Handling of Rejected Returns

Items returned to the Company that fall under these exclusions will be held for 14 days. The customer will be notified via email and offered the option to have the item shipped back to them at a flat-rate shipping cost of £5.95. If the customer does not respond or refuses to pay for the return shipping within this window, the Company will dispose of the item in an environmentally responsible manner.

### 3.1 Definition of Bespoke Goods

This section applies to all products categorized as "Custom-Tailored," "Personalized," or "Made-to-Order" (MTO). These include, but are not limited to, apparel with custom embroidery, bespoke measurements, or specific material configurations selected by the customer through the OmniRetail UK "Design Studio" interface.

### 3.2 Exclusion from Cancellation Rights

Pursuant to the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, specifically Regulation 28(1)(b), the statutory right to cancel does not apply to "the supply of goods that are made to the consumer’s specifications or are clearly personalised." Consequently, once an order for a custom-tailored item has entered the "Production" phase (typically 2 hours after order placement), it cannot be cancelled, amended, or refunded for reasons of style preference, size changes, or change of mind.

### 3.3 Defective Upon Arrival (DUA) Exceptions

The only exception to the non-refundable status of bespoke goods is if the item is "Defective Upon Arrival." A defect is defined as:

1. A significant deviation from the measurements provided by the customer (tolerance of +/- 1.5cm is standard in the textile industry and does not constitute a defect).
2. Incorrect personalization (e.g., misspelling of a name that differs from the customer's input).
3. Structural manufacturing faults (e.g., failed seams, fabric tears).

### 3.4 Reporting and Validation

Customers must report defects in bespoke items within 48 hours of delivery. Detailed high-resolution photographs showing the defect alongside the original order specifications must be uploaded to the OmniRetail UK Returns Portal. Upon validation by our Quality Assurance (QA) team, the Company will, at its discretion, either provide a professional alteration service at no cost or manufacture a replacement item. Refunds for bespoke items are only issued if a replacement cannot be produced within a reasonable timeframe (30 days).

### 5.1 Inspection and Approval

Upon receipt of the returned goods at our National Distribution Centre (NDC), the items undergo a mandatory inspection process. This process typically takes 3 to 5 business days. Our technicians verify the condition of the product, the presence of all accessories, and the integrity of the serial numbers. Once the inspection is complete, an "Approval" or "Rejection" notification is sent to the customer via email.

### 5.2 Refund Timelines

Approved refunds are processed within 14 calendar days of the date the Company receives the goods, in compliance with the Consumer Contracts Regulations.

- **Credit/Debit Cards:** Funds typically appear in the customer's account within 3-5 business days after processing, depending on the issuing bank.
- **PayPal:** Refunds are usually visible in the PayPal balance within 24 hours of processing.
- **Klarna / Buy-Now-Pay-Later:** The Company will notify the provider of the return. The provider will then adjust the customer's statement or issue a refund for installments already paid. This can take up to 10 business days.

### 5.3 Store Credit Option

Customers may opt for "OmniCredit" store credit instead of a refund to the original payment method. OmniCredit is issued instantly upon the approval of the return and includes a bonus £5.00 incentive for future purchases. OmniCredit is non-refundable and expires 12 months from the date of issue.

### 5.4 Restocking Fees

For high-value electronics (items exceeding £1,000.00 in value), a 10% restocking fee applies if the item is returned for a change of mind and has been opened. This fee covers the cost of technical re-certification and the loss of value associated with selling the item as "Open Box" or "Refurbished." This fee is waived if the item is returned factory-sealed or is found to be defective under the terms of the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

### 6.1 Standard Delivery Framework

OmniRetail UK Ltd offers a tiered shipping structure designed to balance cost-efficiency with reliable delivery timelines. Our primary logistics partners for Mainland UK are Royal Mail (Tracked 48) and DPD. "Mainland UK" is defined as England, Wales, and the parts of Scotland excluding the Highlands and Islands.

### 6.2 Shipping Rates and Thresholds

- **Standard Delivery (Orders ≥ £50.00):** Free of charge.
- **Standard Delivery (Orders < £50.00):** A flat fee of £3.99 is applied at checkout.
  The order value threshold is calculated based on the net total after any discounts or promotional codes are applied, and before the addition of shipping fees.

### 6.3 Service Level Agreement (SLA)

For Standard Delivery, the Company targets a delivery window of 2 to 4 business days from the date of dispatch. "Business days" are defined as Monday through Friday, excluding UK Bank Holidays. While 95% of orders meet this SLA, the Company does not guarantee these timelines for standard shipping. During peak seasonal periods (e.g., Black Friday, December), the SLA may be extended to 5 to 7 business days.

### 6.4 Dispatch Notification and Tracking

Orders are typically dispatched within 24 hours of payment authorization. Upon dispatch, the customer will receive an automated "Shipping Confirmation" email containing a unique tracking number and a link to the carrier’s real-time tracking portal. It is the customer's responsibility to monitor this tracking and ensure someone is available to receive the parcel. If a delivery is unsuccessful, the carrier will typically attempt a second delivery on the next business day or leave the parcel at a designated local pickup point.

OmniRetail UK Ltd handles all necessary documentation to ensure a "Green Lane" experience for the customer. ### 9.1 Definition of Lost in Transit

An item is officially classified as "Lost in Transit" if it has not been delivered within 7 business days after the estimated delivery date and the tracking information has not been updated for at least 72 hours. Customers cannot initiate a lost parcel claim until this 7-day "carrier buffer" has elapsed.

### 9.2 Investigation Workflow

Upon notification of a missing parcel, OmniRetail UK Ltd will initiate a formal investigation with the carrier. This process takes up to 5 business days. The investigation includes:

1. Verification of the GPS geolocation coordinates of the delivery scan.
2. Review of the "Proof of Delivery" (POD) photograph.
3. Interview with the attending courier.

### 9.3 Delivery Disputes (Marked as Delivered but Not Received)

If a parcel is marked as "Delivered" but the customer claims non-receipt, the Company will rely on the carrier's GPS and photographic evidence. If the GPS coordinates match the customer's delivery address and the POD photo shows the parcel at the correct doorstep or in a designated "Safe Place" previously authorized by the customer via the carrier's app, the Company’s liability ends. In such cases, the customer is advised to contact local law enforcement to report parcel theft.

### 9.4 Resolution of Valid Claims

If the carrier investigation confirms the parcel is lost or was delivered to an incorrect address due to courier error, the Company will offer the customer a choice between:

1. A one-time free replacement of the entire order (subject to stock availability).
2. A full refund to the original payment method.
   The Company reserves the right to require a signed "Denial of Receipt" (DOR) legal declaration from the customer before a replacement or refund is issued. Providing false information on a DOR form constitutes fraud and will be reported to Action Fraud.

### 11.1 Scope of Coverage

OmniRetail UK Ltd warrants that all consumer electronics and smart devices sold under the "Omni" brand or through our authorized distribution channels will be free from defects in materials and workmanship under normal use for a period of 12 months from the date of original retail purchase. This warranty is provided in addition to, and does not affect, the customer’s statutory rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

### 11.2 Statutory vs. Commercial Warranty

Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, goods must be as described, fit for purpose, and of satisfactory quality. During the first 6 months, any fault is presumed to have existed at the time of delivery unless the Company can prove otherwise. Between 6 months and 6 years (5 years in Scotland), the burden of proof shifts to the customer to prove the fault was present at the time of delivery. The OmniRetail 12-Month Limited Warranty simplifies this by providing a commercial guarantee that covers hardware failures regardless of the burden of proof, provided the failure is not caused by external factors.

### 11.3 Warranty Claim Requirements

To invoke a warranty claim, the customer must:

1. Provide the original Proof of Purchase.
2. Ensure the device’s unique IMEI or Serial Number is legible and has not been tampered with.
3. Provide a detailed description of the hardware failure.
   The warranty is non-transferable and applies only to the original purchaser of the product.

### 11.4 Remedy Options

If a valid hardware defect is identified within the 12-month period, the Company will, at its sole discretion:

1. Repair the hardware using new or refurbished parts that are equivalent to new in performance and reliability.
2. Replace the product with an identical model or a product with equivalent functionality.
3. Refund the purchase price (subject to depreciation based on the period of use if the claim occurs after the first 6 months).

### 12.1 The 48-Hour Reporting Window

Items that are non-functional upon first use or show visible physical damage upon unboxing are classified as "Dead-On-Arrival" (DOA) or "Transit Damaged." Customers must report such instances to the OmniRetail UK Support Team within 48 hours of the delivery timestamp recorded by the carrier. Failure to report damage within this window creates a legal presumption that the damage occurred while the product was in the customer's possession.

### 12.2 Evidence Requirements

To process a DOA or Transit Damage claim, the customer must provide the following via our secure upload portal:

1. High-resolution photographs of the external shipping box (showing any dents, punctures, or moisture stains).
2. Photographs of the internal packaging and the specific damage to the product.
3. A short video (max 30 seconds) demonstrating the failure to power on or the functional defect.

### 12.3 Expedited DOA Replacement

Once the evidence is reviewed and the claim is preliminary approved (usually within 24 hours), the Company will arrange for a priority collection of the damaged unit. Unlike standard warranty repairs, DOA cases are eligible for "Advanced Replacement" where a new unit is dispatched immediately upon the carrier's scan of the returned item, ensuring the customer is not without a working product for an extended period.

### 12.4 Exclusions to DOA

The DOA policy does not apply to:

- Minor cosmetic imperfections on the packaging that do not affect the product.
- Software configuration issues or "user-error" setup difficulties (these are handled by Technical Support).
- Items purchased as "Grade B" or "Refurbished" where cosmetic wear was disclosed at the time of sale.

### 13.1 Non-Covered Events

The OmniRetail UK Limited Warranty is strictly limited to manufacturing defects. It does not cover damage resulting from external factors. Specifically, the warranty is voided under the following conditions:

The warranty does not cover natural degradation that does not affect the functionality of the device. This includes:

- Scratches, dents, or scuffs to the chassis.

The Company is not responsible for any data loss, software corruption, or loss of business profits resulting from a hardware failure. ### 14.1 Definition of Consumable Parts

Consumable parts are components designed to diminish in performance over time through regular use. This primarily includes rechargeable Lithium-Ion (Li-ion) batteries, but also applies to printer toner, projector bulbs, and certain mechanical filters.

### 14.2 Battery Health Benchmarks

All rechargeable batteries lose capacity over time. A battery is not considered "defective" simply because its runtime has decreased. For the purposes of the 12-month warranty, a battery defect is defined as:

1. The battery failing to hold more than 80% of its original rated capacity within the first 12 months, provided the cycle count is under 300.
2. Sudden shutdowns when the battery indicator shows remaining charge.
3. Physical swelling or expansion of the battery cell (which requires immediate cessation of use).

### 14.3 Normal Aging vs. Defect

If a battery’s capacity is above 80% or if the device has exceeded 300 charge cycles, any decrease in performance is classified as "Normal Wear and Tear" and is not covered by the warranty. Customers can request a battery replacement service, which will be quoted at a fixed price including parts, labor, and return shipping.

### 14.4 Proper Battery Maintenance

To preserve warranty eligibility, customers are expected to follow battery health best practices:

- Avoid exposing the device to temperatures exceeding 35°C (95°F).
- Do not leave the device in a state of 0% charge for extended periods (which can lead to deep discharge failure).
- Use only the original OmniRetail UK supplied charger or a certified USB-PD (Power Delivery) equivalent.
  Failure to adhere to these guidelines, resulting in "Deep Discharge" or "Thermal Stress" failure, will result in the warranty claim being rejected.

### 15.1 The RMA Process

Once a warranty claim is initiated, the customer will be issued a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number. The customer must ship the device to the OmniRetail UK Technical Centre. The Company provides a tracked shipping label for all valid warranty claims.

### 15.2 Bench Test and Diagnosis

Upon arrival, the device enters the "Bench Test" phase. Our technicians perform a comprehensive diagnostic suite to replicate the reported fault. This phase is completed within 3 business days of receipt. If the fault cannot be replicated ("No Fault Found" or NFF), the device will be returned to the customer, and a £25.00 diagnostic and shipping fee may be charged.

### 15.3 Repair Service Level Agreement (SLA)

The Company aims to complete all hardware repairs within 14 working days of the diagnostic approval. This SLA includes the sourcing of parts and final quality control testing. If a repair is expected to exceed 21 days due to global parts shortages, the Company will offer the customer a replacement unit of equal or better specifications.

### 15.4 Replacement Dispatch

If a device is deemed "Beyond Economical Repair" (BER), a replacement will be issued.

- Replacements are typically "Service Units" which may be new or "As-New" refurbished units that have undergone rigorous testing.
- The replacement unit inherits the remaining warranty period of the original device or is provided with a 90-day warranty, whichever is longer.
- The Company does not guarantee that the replacement will be the same color as the original, though every effort will be made to match it.

### 15.5 Out-of-Warranty (OOW) Repairs

For devices outside the 12-month window or with excluded damage (e.g., cracked screens), the Company offers an OOW Repair Service. A non-refundable inspection fee of £35.00 is required, which is then deducted from the final repair quote if the customer chooses to proceed.

### 16.1 Price Integrity Principle

OmniRetail UK Ltd operates on a "Fixed Price" model to ensure fairness and transparency for all customers. The prices displayed on the website at the time of purchase are final. The Company does not engage in price haggling, and Customer Service Representatives (CSRs) are strictly prohibited from offering discretionary manual discounts to close a sale or appease a dissatisfied customer.

### 16.2 Prohibition of Discretionary Discounts

CSRs do not have the technical capability or the legal authorization to modify the unit price of an item within the ERP system. Any attempt to bypass this via "Ghost Vouchers" or manual credit adjustments is flagged by our internal audit software, "OmniGuard." Unauthorized price overrides are considered a breach of employment contract and may result in disciplinary action for the employee and the cancellation of the customer's order.

### 16.3 Discount Gap Detection

The Company utilizes automated Discount Gap Detection (DGD) algorithms. If an order is processed at a price that deviates from the Daily Master Price List (DMPL) without a valid, system-generated promotional code, the order is automatically placed on "Finance Hold." The customer will be contacted to pay the difference, or the order will be cancelled and refunded in full.

### 16.4 Errors and Omissions

In the event of a "Manifest Error" (e.g., a £1,000 laptop listed for £10.00 due to a technical glitch), the Company is not legally obligated to honor the price. Pursuant to the doctrine of "Unilateral Mistake" in UK Contract Law, if the price is so low that the customer ought to have known it was an error, no contract is formed. The Company reserves the right to cancel such orders prior to dispatch, even if an order confirmation has been sent.

### 19.1 Voucher Code Issuance

OmniRetail UK Ltd issues various types of promotional codes, including "Welcome" codes, "Birthday" codes, and "Recovery" codes (issued by CS for service failures). Each code is subject to specific Terms and Conditions (T&Cs) which are linked at the footer of the promotional email.

### 19.2 Anti-Stacking Policy

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, the Company operates a "Single Code" policy. Only one promotional voucher or discount code can be applied to a single transaction. If a customer attempts to use multiple codes, the system will automatically apply the code that offers the highest monetary value and invalidate the others.

### 19.3 Minimum Cart Spend Calculation

Many voucher codes (e.g., "£10 off when you spend £100") are subject to a Minimum Cart Spend (MCS). The MCS is calculated based on the:

- Subtotal of eligible items (excluding "Clearance" or "Non-Discountable" items).
- Exclusion of shipping fees and gift wrap charges.
  If a customer returns part of an order, causing the remaining total to fall below the MCS, the value of the voucher will be deducted from the refund. For example, if a £10 discount was applied to a £100 order, and the customer returns a £20 item (bringing the net spend to £80), the £10 discount is forfeited, and the customer will only receive a £10 refund.

### 19.4 Expiry and Extensions

All voucher codes have a hard expiry date. Once a code has expired, it cannot be reactivated or replaced. CSRs are not authorized to "extend" the validity of a promotional campaign for individual customers. Codes have no cash value and are not transferable.

### 20.1 Identification of Promotional Fraud

OmniRetail UK Ltd reserves the right to cancel any order where there is a reasonable suspicion of promotional fraud. This includes:

- Creating multiple accounts to reuse "First Order" or "Refer-a-Friend" codes.
- Exploiting technical glitches to stack non-stackable discounts.
- Using automated "bots" to sweep limited-time promotional stock.
  Accounts found engaging in these activities will be blacklisted from the platform.

### 20.2 Order Cancellation Rights

The Company reserves the right to cancel any order at any time prior to the "Dispatched" status. A contract is only formed when the Company sends a "Shipping Confirmation" email. If an order is cancelled due to suspected fraud or pricing errors, a full refund will be issued to the original payment method, and a notification will be sent to the customer within 24 hours.

### 20.3 Disputed Chargebacks

A chargeback is a forced reversal of funds initiated by the customer through their bank. OmniRetail UK Ltd views "Friendly Fraud" (initiating a chargeback for a delivered and functional item) as a serious matter.

- **Evidence Submission:** In the event of a chargeback, the Company will provide the bank with a "Compelling Evidence" package, including the IP address used at checkout, signed Proof of Delivery (POD) with GPS coordinates, and all communication logs.
- **Account Suspension:** Any account associated with a chargeback will be immediately suspended. To reinstate the account, the customer must withdraw the chargeback or pay the disputed amount plus a £25.00 administrative fee.

### 20.4 Legal Recourse

For high-value chargebacks (exceeding £500.00), the Company reserves the right to pursue the debt through the UK Small Claims Court and report the incident to Credit Reference Agencies, which may impact the customer's credit rating. We encourage all customers to resolve disputes through our 24/7 Customer Support team before contacting their bank.
